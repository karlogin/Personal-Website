Background

I'm a passionate Game Developer and Designer with over 10 years of experience building games from the ground up — designing, architecting, testing, and shipping features across console, PC, mobile, and VR. Currently a Principal Software Engineer at Zynga in Toronto.

Previously Lead Unity Developer at Gameloft, shipping titles on Apple Arcade, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch. Before that, Lead Developer at SilverbackVR, focused on iOS and Oculus Quest. I hold an MSc in Computing & Data Analytics from Saint Mary's University, Halifax.

My mission is to work in challenging, innovative environments where I can apply deep technical skills and lead high-performing teams to create exceptional gaming experiences.
